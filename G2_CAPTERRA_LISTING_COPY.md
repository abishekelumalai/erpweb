## Long description
*(Full paragraph(s) — main listing body)*

> ChaloSchools is a school management ERP built specifically for K-12 schools in India, replacing spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, and paper registers with one integrated platform. It covers the full operational lifecycle of a school — Admission Management, Student Management, Staff Management, Fee Management, AI-powered Timetable & Scheduling, Attendance Management, Academic & Examination, and Communication Management — plus add-on modules for Academic Inventory Tracking, Payroll, Library Management, Transport Management, Inspace Performance Insights, and an AI Secretary built for school leadership.
>
> The platform includes three purpose-built mobile apps: Chaloschools for parents, AnsApp for teachers, and C-365 for school management — each giving its audience exactly the tools they need, not a one-size-fits-all interface.
>
> ChaloSchools is ISO 27001:2022 certified and built in line with India's DPDP Act 2023 data protection principles, with OTP-verified changes, role-based access control, and personal data masked by default. It's backed by Inspace Technologies, bringing enterprise-grade IT practices — encryption, multi-tenant isolation, point-in-time backup, 99.9% uptime — to K-12 schools.
>
> ChaloSchools currently serves 200+ schools across 4 countries, impacting 1.5M+ students, and supports Pre School, State & CBSE, IB, Cambridge, Montessori, and Matriculation/Higher Education boards and curricula.
